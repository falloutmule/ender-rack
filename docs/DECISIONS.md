# EnderRack durable decisions

Current as of 2026-09-04; supersedes conflicting historical status language, not historical evidence.

1. Mechanical donor is the original/older Ender-3-family X-axis. It was NEVER disassembled. Aquila identifies the print machine only.
2. Community Ender-3 V2 fit geometry is retired. Official Creality E-plate + scoped R2 physical evidence establish the interface and seating face.
3. Preserve interfaces, physical facts and known failures independently from disposable coupon solids. Required empty clearances do not require reproducing R2 pocket shelves.
4. R5 wrong-face relationship, 22.25 mm stand-off and legacy V-notch are retired. R6/R6R1 architecture is rejected. R6R2 digital PASS has an owner-waived physical witness, never physical PASS.
5. Current article is R7_ARCHITECTURE_V2_Candidate_001, with two named exterior-open access windows, fixed selector axes/orientation and +15 mm candidate stand-off. This documentation task changes no geometry.
6. R7 V2 is printed and passes the observed carriage seating/interface scope. Unsupported boss-clearance overhang print quality is defective but did not prevent seating. No selector exists physically; full R7 physical PASS is outstanding.
7. Keep digital checks, construction assertions, physical evidence and audit caveats separate. An invalid source gear stays UNVERIFIED, never zero collision. Earlier broad master reproducibility claims need the upgraded-skill re-audit before production.
8. The 0.25 mm demonstrated weak-ligament failure is known bad; 5 mm is a design preference, not an invented universal minimum. A passing STEP is not strength proof.
9. Use existing verifier v0.2 only for its supported operators. No verifier expansion, CAD redesign or audit rerun in this selector-build task.
10. Build one stock default moving selector: 9 mm C-cart middle, 10x10 two-hole right, Micro-Fit wiring holder, FT1117M servo and conventional BMG filament-drive mechanism. Keep the filament-drive motor; omit the replaced stock MGN translation system.
11. Source authority is pinned TradRack `f89dc0b115adfc49195d782c5d7ecd348a574891`, its default STEP, STL files and official manual; the linked live BOM snapshot is separately dated/hashed. Unresolved discrepancies must remain visible.
12. Stock selector printing follows upstream ASA/PC guidance, not PLA coupon settings. R7 service sequence: mount/tighten carriage adapter first, then mount selector finger-snug using verified engagement.
13. Full R7 physical PASS gates production adapter and 238 mm sweep. Target 15 lanes at 17 mm pitch; 12-lane fallback. Context lane views are not a collision sweep.
14. Read actual repository HEAD/dirty state; preserve user work and history. Happy Hare/Klipper and donor electronics remain future plans, not commissioned hardware.
