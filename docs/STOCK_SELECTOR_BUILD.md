### Procurement and assembly limits — do not guess

- **R7 mounting screws: UNRESOLVED length/engagement.** Four selector mounting positions remain, but stock MGN M3x8 screws are not automatically correct for R7's provisional blind pilots. Measure the real selector counterbore/grip and available R7 engagement before choosing these four screws. Do not drive until bottoming, drill through, retap, or change R7 as part of this task. Existing R7-to-Ender fasteners remain in use; the donor has never been taken apart for this task.
- **BMG source detail:** the default CAD uses a `sherpa_mini_gear_50t_sl_7mm` occurrence and a separate 0.5 mm internal shim. Its gear solid is invalid and remains UNVERIFIED. The BOM and manual call for the conventional BMG shaft assembly. Manufacturer [15021](https://www.bondtech.se/product/shaft-assembly/) confirms that original assembly, not a modern integrated replacement. Exact internal-shim supply and dimensional equivalence to the source Sherpa-named subassembly are **UNRESOLVED**; ask the supplier to confirm the complete original assembly contents before ordering a substitute or a bare shaft. The four standalone 5x7 shims above do not silently replace this internal shim. A current Sherpa internals kit is not authenticated as an interchangeable purchase.
- **Microswitch screw CAD catalog label:** source says `PHT 2x13x12.2`, whereas the BOM and build instruction explicitly specify M2x12. The table follows the two explicit procurement/assembly sources; the catalog-label dimensional discrepancy remains UNRESOLVED, not a claimed exact CAD match.
- **Inserts:** the default contains 17 moving-root insert occurrences. Twelve serve this immediate build; two are chain attachment and three optional board attachment. Install active inserts only; do not infer that the right-carriage label “With Breakout Board” requires buying a PCB. The selected source actually contains the six Micro-Fit housings. Generic long kit inserts are not the specified short D5xL4 inserts.
- Local switch leads and consumables are needed to complete wiring, not to prove static fit. Upstream wiring guidance gives approximate cut lengths (motor 210 mm, servo 170 mm, switch 155 mm); dry-route before cutting. Use motor/servo supplied leads and two switch leads; exact purchased wire length, heat-shrink size and final harness length are **UNRESOLVED until routed**, not a made-up selector BOM quantity. No controller hookup or powered calibration in this task.

## REUSE CHECK — DO THIS BEFORE BUYING MOTOR OR BMG PARTS

The two mirrored/reverse BMG housings are irrelevant if their removable internals match. Record both clones separately; do not assume they contain identical parts.

| Candidate | Measure/inspect | Acceptance comparison |
|---|---|---|
| spare Ender stepper | 42 mm face; mounting-hole center spacing; shaft diameter; shaft protrusion from face; body depth; connector/pin order; rated current/phase; smooth shaft/bearing condition | Must match NEMA17 mounting, 5 mm motor-gear bore and the stock 48 mm envelope/gear alignment without R7 or selector interference. Torque/current suitability must be supported by the motor label/datasheet, not appearance. |
| 17T motor pinion | tooth count; 5 mm bore; face width; set-screw presence/condition; installed axial position range | Must mesh with the 50T gear at the stock motor spacing and retain securely on the spare motor shaft. |
| primary hob/50T geared shaft | 50T gear; 5 mm bearing journals; hob diameter and groove; overall/shoulder lengths; set screw; runout/damage | Compare the complete axial stack to the official conventional BMG shaft assembly. Do not approve a short/integrated shaft by gear appearance alone. |
| idler drive gear | 3 mm needle-bearing bore; hob diameter/groove alignment; width; tooth profile; damage | Must accept the 3 x 20 pin and two needle bearings and align its filament groove with the primary hob. |
| needle bearings | quantity two; fit on 3 mm pin; OD/length; smoothness/play | Both must fit the idler gear without binding or visible corrosion/brinelling. |
| 3 mm pin | diameter at several points; length; straightness and surface finish | Target is 3 x 20 mm; reject bent/scored or loose parts. |
| spring/plastic washer | spring OD, wire diameter, free length and damage; washer ID/OD/thickness | Compare physically with the BMG thumbscrew preload stack; reuse only if it seats squarely and gives usable adjustment with M3x30/collar. |

## ASSEMBLE — stock selector only, no powered motion

1. Print the twelve parts and two tools in the official stock material/process. Inspect holes and remove support/first-layer defects without changing interfaces. Lay out hardware. Prepare the 12 active short inserts in their mapped parts; follow PDF pp24,30,35,40,42. Do not insert the five deferred chain/board inserts just because CAD contains them.
2. Bench-assemble the middle with the 4 mm ball, the lever filament switch and two M2x12 screws (pp27–29). Solder to the outer switch terminals before installation if preparing its leads. Do not perform the manual's MGN rail mounting steps.
3. Press one MR115 into the left carriage and join left to middle with two M3x8 (pp29–30). Prepare the motor plate with its three inserts and one MR85.
4. Fit the 5x40 pivot shaft to the motor plate, approximately 6 mm rear protrusion per p31. Attach the filament motor and spacer using three M3x10 and three washers.
5. Assemble the conventional BMG geared shaft, primary filament gear and 17T motor pinion (p32). Retain the correct set screws; establish alignment, not force. Resolve the internal-shim procurement caveat before completing that stack.
6. Fit left casing, 3x20 shaft, two needle bearings and secondary/idler gear (pp33–34). Add arm, shims and right casing/MR85 in the sequence of pp35–36. Four standalone shims are available; the manual treats a casing shim as optional depending on fit. Do not force all shims into a binding mechanism.
7. Clamp casing with three M3x25, adjust motor gear mesh for free movement, then fit spring and BMG plastic washer on M3x30 with printed tensioner collar (pp37–38). Do not use the kit's thumbscrew instead of the collar construction.
8. Fit the tilting unit and pivot shim into the left bearing; fit right MR115 and right carriage, attaching with two M3x8 (pp39–41). Check free pivoting.
9. Assemble printed horn with its insert, M3x10 clamp screw/washer and 623 bearing on the other M3x10 (p42). Install FT1117M with lower M3x10 and upper M3x16 shared with the Micro-Fit holder (pp43–44). Retain the selected M2.5x5 spline screw but do not force horn angle; final angular setting needs the upstream jigs/calibration later.
10. Fit the three plug housings into the holder and their mating receptacles as required. Install ECAS output and cable cover with its M3x16 (p58). Leave chain, optional PCB and controller wiring out. Check drive rotation, idler movement, sensor-ball action and tilting movement by hand without forcing the servo gears.
11. Keep R7 on the intact Ender carriage: seat R7, then tighten its carriage fasteners before attaching the selector. Resolve four R7 selector-screw lengths/engagement as noted above; do not assume stock rail screws work. Attach the complete selector finger-snug only.
12. Record carriage seating/four-hole alignment; selector mount/orientation; measured real stand-off; rocking, translation, rotation; wheel/eccentric and real belt/clamp clearance; fastener/tool access; light hand-load stiffness. Do not power motion. Only the complete scoped R7 physical result can unlock production and the 238 mm sweep.
