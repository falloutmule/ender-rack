Status: **PHYSICAL FIT-CHECK PASS — 2026-09-01**

The photographed R2 coupon seated on the real Ender carriage plate with its validated hole pattern aligned and its two boss-clearance pockets accepting the retained raised hotend features. This promotes the official E plate only as the controlling fabricated-fit reference for the physically exercised interface features:

- hole-pattern position and orientation;
- steel seating face;
- R2 V-notch = tensioner/idler side;
- R2 cross = motor side;
- boss-clearance relationship used by R2.

It does not physically validate every official E-plate contour, the belt datum, wheel stack depth, or any unencountered feature. The bosses remain clearance features, not precision locating datums. Location is controlled by the validated fastener pattern and steel contact face.

The successful relief geometry is **two pockets, diameter 5.5 mm × 5.2 mm deep**. The 5 × 5 mm values used to derive those pockets are nominal successful coupon inputs, not independently measured boss dimensions.
